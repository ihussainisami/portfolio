About Me

I build practical AI automation systems that help businesses manage leads, enquiries, CRM workflows, and repetitive processes with less manual effort.

My work includes lead segmentation and AI scoring, lead capture and CRM automation, AI-powered support ticket routing, and a WhatsApp assistant designed for real estate enquiries and appointment booking.

Every workflow is built with reliability in mind. I include validation, fallback paths, verification steps, and human alerts where they matter, so automation does not silently fail.

I work with n8n, AI integrations, APIs, webhooks, CRM workflows, Airtable, Google Sheets, Gmail, Slack, and WhatsApp integrations.

I am particularly interested in building automation for real estate teams that need to respond to enquiries faster, qualify leads, and reduce manual follow-up.
